**Project Overview:**
This custom Jigglypuff trading card was my very first hands-on design project created entirely in Figma! Built as part of the **Codédex UI/UX Course**, this project served as a fun, creative sandbox to explore Figma’s primary toolset, visual hierarchy, and component structuring.

## Objectives

1. Master essential Figma tools, including frames, vector paths, layers, and color palettes.
2. Recreate an iconic Pokémon trading card aesthetic while practicing layout alignment and typography.
3. Apply fundamental UI/UX concepts learned during the Codédex course to craft a clean, polished digital design asset.

## Highlights & Features

1. **Card Layout & Structure:**
   - Designed using precise Figma frames to match classic trading card proportions.
   - Structured with organized layer groups for easy component management.

2. **Jigglypuff Aesthetic & Color Palette:**
   - Curated pink color scheme tailored specifically to match Jigglypuff's visual identity.
   - Styled health points (HP), attack stats, and move descriptions with custom typography.

3. **Auto Layout & Vectors:**
   - Experimented with Figma's **Auto Layout** feature for balanced element spacing.
   - Modeled vector shapes and icons to give the card an authentic, polished finish.

## Tools & Skills Used

- **Design Tool:** Figma
- **Learning Platform:** Codédex (UI/UX Design Course)
- **Core Concepts:** Vector editing, Auto Layout, Color theory, Visual hierarchy, Component layering

## Takeaways

Designing this Jigglypuff card was an incredibly fun milestone. It made learning Figma's interface feel completely intuitive and sparked a huge interest in diving deeper into UI/UX design and creative digital projects!
